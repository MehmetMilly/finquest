# FinQuest demo script

Production URL: https://finquest-app-mu.vercel.app/

## 90-second judge demo

**0–10s — Thesis**

“FinQuest helps Saudi young adults try a financial commitment before signing it. The math is deterministic; the future-self companion explains the trade-off and turns it into a small quest.”

On onboarding, point out **personal data** and **Sara demo**. Use Sara for speed.

**10–25s — Personalized starting point**

On **اليوم**, show Sara's monthly room, savings rate, commitments, emergency runway, and explicit synthetic goal. Say: “These all come from one active profile—not a fixed analysis animation.”

**25–45s — Decision**

Open **القرارات → شراء سيارة**. Change the price or deposit. Point to:

- installment;
- final payment;
- effective monthly vehicle obligation including its reserve;
- pressure relative to the profile's income.

Choose **شوف أثر القرار**.

**45–65s — Data analysis**

Switch between the three futures. Show monthly room, emergency runway, named-goal timing, and 2036 net worth. First point out the unchanged baseline, then deliberately choose one sensitivity preset and explain that it reruns the same engine with one changed assumption; it is a scenario, not a prediction.

**65–78s — Grounded AI**

Open Fِي and choose one question. Point to the source badge:

- “شرح AI مبني على الحسابات” when the server key/provider is available; or
- “شرح محلي جاهز” when it is not.

Say: “Every number was already calculated before AI saw the compact facts.”

**78–90s — Gamification and close**

Open **المهام**, select a quest, start it, record a step, and show the completion gate, XP, consistency, badges, and self-report disclosure.

Close: “FinQuest makes the future consequence feel present—without pretending to be a bank, adviser, or prediction engine.”

## Four-minute fallback demo

1. Enter a personal profile and intentionally show one validation message.
2. Revisit the home metrics and show that the entered goal label/amount powers goal timing. If no amount was entered, point out the illustrative label.
3. Explain the car financing fields and balloon reserve.
4. Compare all three paths and show the opportunity-cost chain.
5. Try all three sensitivity presets.
6. Demonstrate the deployed AI explanation once, then explain the equally usable missing-key/provider fallback without repeatedly spending provider calls.
7. Progress and complete the buffer quest; return home and show that its modeled savings effect appears only after completion.
8. Refresh to prove persistence.
9. Open assumptions/account, point out the disclosed 12-month debt approximation, then reset and show clean onboarding.

## Demo reset

Use **حسابي → ابدأ من جديد** or the assumptions sheet reset action. This clears the app state, active profile, and saved car inputs on the device.
