# Human user-test plan

No human interviews or usability sessions are claimed in this repository. Browser checks in `.agent/reports/` are heuristic agent testing only.

## Participants

Recruit 3–5 Saudi young adults, ideally ages 18–30, with a mix of people considering a car and people who are not. Do not collect bank statements, account credentials, national IDs, or exact debt contracts.

## 20-minute session

1. Ask the participant to describe what they think FinQuest does from the welcome screen.
2. Let them choose personal or Sara mode without coaching.
3. Ask them to test a car offer and explain the difference between installment and effective monthly vehicle obligation.
4. Ask them to compare the three futures and identify which assumption they trust least.
5. Ask them to use a sensitivity preset and explain what changed.
6. Ask one question through Fِي and check whether they understand AI versus calculated figures.
7. Ask them to start, progress, and complete a quest; verify they know it is self-reported and no money moved.
8. Ask them to reset the experience.

## Observe

- Time to articulate the product purpose.
- Onboarding abandonment or confusing fields.
- Misinterpretation of balloon payment, obligation, net worth, or “future” as a prediction.
- Whether the avatar feels warm, contemporary, and culturally appropriate.
- Whether conversational Arabic feels natural but credible.
- Missed controls, keyboard/focus problems, overflow, or text collisions.
- Trust in the AI/source labels and disclosures.

## Exit questions

- “What changed in your understanding of the decision?”
- “Which number felt most useful or confusing?”
- “Did anything sound like advice or a guarantee?”
- “Did the character feel appropriate and consistent?”
- “Would any quest feel worth doing in real life, and why?”

Document observations and exact quotes only with consent. Separate findings from team interpretation; never fabricate completion rates from this small formative sample.
